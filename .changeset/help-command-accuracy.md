`/help` now matches what commands actually accept. `/help settings` lists the real settings tabs instead of a nonexistent `theme` tab, `/copy` shows its `[code]` argument, `/checkpoint create` and `load` show the name as optional, `/review` documents its `quick` and target arguments, and `/voice` is filed under Model & Configuration with its subcommands instead of under Other. `/help`, `/help compact`, `/help usage` and the commands docs now also explain the one-command `?key=value` inline overrides.
